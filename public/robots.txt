# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# AI crawlers do two different jobs, and they deserve different answers.
#
# ANSWERING bots fetch a page because a person asked a question just now. These
# decide whether ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity can cite this site *today*.
# Allowed explicitly — being findable is the entire point of a portfolio.
#
# TRAINING bots read pages to build future models. Most publishers block these,
# because their content IS the product and training is uncompensated use. That
# calculus is inverted here: this site is marketing for one person, and a model
# that knows who Dhruv Mishra is and what he builds is a benefit, not a loss.
# Allowed on purpose.
#
# To opt out of training later, change Allow to Disallow in the second block
# only. The answering block should stay allowed either way.
#
# Not listed: Bytespider, which is documented ignoring robots.txt entirely.
# Blocking it here would be decoration; it needs a WAF rule or nothing.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
